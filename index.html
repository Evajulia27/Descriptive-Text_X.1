What is Descriptive Text?

Descriptive text is a text which says what a person, place, or thing is like. Its purpose is to describe and reveal a particular person, place, or thing in vivid detail so the reader can picture it in their mind.

Social Function

To describe a specific object, person, animal, historical monument, or tourist attraction to the reader in sensory detail.

Generic Structure

Consists of two main structural blocks: Identification (introducing the topic) and Description (details, features, & qualities).

Language Features

Uses Simple Present Tense, descriptive Adjectives, Linking Verbs (is, are, feel), and sensory details.

Sample Text: The Magical Raja Ampat

Read the passage below. Click buttons to toggle color highlights!

Raja Ampat is an archipelago located off the northwest tip of Bird's Head Peninsula in Papua, Indonesia. It consists of four main islands surrounded by hundreds of smaller ones.

The waters surrounding Raja Ampat are home to incredible marine life, including thousands of species of colorful fish and vibrant coral reefs. The crystal-clear ocean allows divers to see underwater formations clearly. Visitors from all over the world come to experience its breathtaking natural beauty and peaceful atmosphere.

Generic Structure Breakdown

Every descriptive text is organized into specific parts so the reader can understand the description easily.

PART 1

1. Identification

This paragraph introduces the subject to be described (Who, What, or Where). It provides general background information so readers know what specific object is being focused on.

"Raja Ampat is an archipelago located off the northwest tip of Papua, Indonesia..."
PART 2

2. Description

This part provides specific details about the subject. It describes parts, qualities, unique characteristics, physical appearance, weather, or atmosphere.

"The waters surrounding Raja Ampat are home to incredible marine life..."

Grammar Corner: Key Language Features

Master these essential grammar rules to write clear and beautiful descriptive texts.

1 Simple Present Tense

Factual & General Truths

Descriptive text uses the Simple Present Tense because it describes facts, ongoing characteristics, and general truths that are currently true.

Verbal Sentence Subject + V1 (s/es)

e.g., "The park welcomes thousands of visitors every month."

Nominal Sentence Subject + is/am/are + Adj/Noun

e.g., "The coral reefs are extraordinarily vibrant."

2 Order of Adjectives

OPINION + SIZE + AGE + COLOR + MATERIAL

When using multiple adjectives before a noun, follow the adjective order rule:

Opinion
beautiful
Size
large
Age
ancient
Color
white
Material
sandy
